# Doodle mascot logo, version 2

Created with the built-in image generation tool using version 1 as a design reference. Saved as `romlerk-doodle-logo-v2.png` with a transparent background. This raster concept adds a human doodle character and leaves the original asset available.

## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Redesign the supplied Romlerk bell/check logo into a richer, highly distinctive doodle mascot logo. The current mark is too generic and simple. Keep its reminder-bell and task-check idea and warm ink #1B1915 / burnt ember #C2542A palette, but transform it into an expressive compact illustrative brand emblem. Main composition: a friendly little human character with a small head, unruly ink hair, a joyful calm face, oversized ember sweater, ink trousers and simple warm ivory shoes, leaning sideways and hugging a LARGE illustrated reminder bell. Their curved arms visibly embrace the bell, giving a feeling of relief, care and remembering. Bell is cream #FAF7F1 inside with bold organic ink outline and a large ember checkmark. Person and bell interlock into one unified memorable diagonal silhouette; bell recognizable, character readable, checkmark clear. Use the style of bold playful editorial doodles: irregular hand-drawn brush contours, chunky black shapes, exaggerated human proportions, limited flat fills, organic curves and carefully shaped negative space. Like an expressive human doodle used in a calm productivity mobile app, not a stock line icon. Add just two small hand-drawn ringing strokes above the bell. More character and visual interest than the supplied simple icon, but cohesive as a logo rather than a full busy scene. Maintain strong silhouette, avoid unnecessary thin details, generous breathing space. Single centered square logo emblem. Actual transparent background around artwork; warm cream is permitted as an interior fill of bell, face and shoes. NO text, NO wordmark, NO mockup, NO concept sheet, NO gradients, NO shadows, NO 3D, NO realistic rendering, NO texture, NO extra background objects.
